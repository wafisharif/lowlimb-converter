# Station 5 gates: walking

Written 2026-10-11, before any Station 5 code existed or had run. A copy was posted to the plan doc (version history
timestamps it) before any run.

**Question:** Stations 2–4 compared the models in random static poses. Do they also agree along a real walking
trajectory: muscle lengths and moment arms over the gait cycle (level 1), and the joint moments needed to walk with
the measured ground reaction forces (level 2)? Level 3 (open-loop forward replay of muscle excitations) is measured
and reported only, because both simulators are expected to drift apart.

## Models and data

gait10dof18musc and gait2354 (both passed Stations 2–4), the generic models used in Stations 2–4.

| Column | MuJoCo file | Run in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MyoConverter (baseline) | `baseline/<model>/<model>_cvt3.xml` (Station 0 output) | MuJoCo 2.3.7 |
| Ours | cvt3 from the full pipeline with the current converter, regenerated by the run script | MuJoCo 3.15 |

Walking data: OpenSim's own example data in opensim-models @ d9b05d4 (`Pipelines/`), the same subject for both models:

| Model | Coordinates (IK) | Ground reaction forces | Time range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| gait10dof18musc | `Gait10dof18musc/OutputReference/IK/subject01_walk_IK.mot` | `Gait10dof18musc/ExperimentalData/subject01_walk_grf.xml` (+ `.mot`) | whole file |
| gait2354 | `Gait2354_Simbody/subject01_walk1_ik.mot` | `Gait2354_Simbody/subject01_walk1_grf.xml` (+ `.mot`) | 0.4–1.6 s (its ID setup) |

The data were recorded for a scaled subject model and are replayed on the generic models. That doesn't matter here:
both simulators get exactly the same inputs, and the question is whether they give the same outputs.

## Method (fixed now)

- **Coordinates:** each IK coordinate low-pass filtered at 6 Hz (as in the pipelines' ID setups), then speeds and
  accelerations from OpenSim's GCV splines of the filtered values. Computed once on the OpenSim side; MuJoCo gets
  the same numbers. Coordinates the model has but the file doesn't are held at their default value (none expected).
- **Dependent MuJoCo joints** (knee translations, moving path points, helper joints): position, speed and
  acceleration from their joint-equality polynomials and the chain rule, so the motion satisfies every equality.
- **Level 1, muscle paths:** at every frame, each muscle's length and its moment arm about every coordinate it
  spans, as in Station 3 (OpenSim: `getLength`, `computeMomentArm`; MuJoCo: tendon length, moment arm = −dL/dq by
  central difference h = 1e-6 with dependent joints re-solved). A pair is scored if OpenSim's peak |moment arm| over
  the trajectory is ≥ 1 mm. Error: RMS over the frames, per muscle (length) and per pair (moment arm), in mm.
- **Level 2, inverse dynamics:** OpenSim: the model plus the ground reaction forces as ExternalLoads,
  `InverseDynamicsSolver` with the filtered speeds and accelerations, muscles excluded (as in the pipelines' ID
  setups). MuJoCo: the same state, `mj_inverse` with constraints disabled (the motion already satisfies them), minus
  the generalized force of the same ground reaction forces (`mj_applyFT` at the same points), then each dependent
  joint's force projected onto its coordinate (virtual work: τ_coordinate = Σ ∂q_joint/∂q_coordinate · τ_joint).
  The MuJoCo model is used exactly as converted (joint damping, virtual-body masses and all). Error: RMS over the
  frames per coordinate, in N·m/kg (N/kg for translations), normalised by OpenSim's total body mass.
- **Level 3, forward replay (reported only):** from the first frame's state, OpenSim and MuJoCo simulate with the
  same muscle excitations (OpenSim CMC result for the pipeline, open loop) and the same ground reaction forces applied
  as external loads. Reported: time until any hip, knee or ankle angle differs by more than 5° between the two.

## Gates

- **W0 THE CHECK IS VALID (every model, both columns):** every OpenSim coordinate has a MuJoCo joint of the same name
  and every IK coordinate is an OpenSim coordinate; every OpenSim muscle has a MuJoCo tendon and actuator of the same
  name; OpenSim's QErr ≤ 1e-9 and MuJoCo's equality residual ≤ 1e-9 in every frame (the motion holds exactly);
  moment-arm self-checks as Station 3's M0 (OpenSim analytic vs finite difference agree within 1e-5 m on ≥ 99% of
  samples; MuJoCo finite differences with h and 10h agree within 1e-6 m on ≥ 99%); all forces finite. If W0 fails,
  W1–W3 are not evaluated.
- **W1 MUSCLE LENGTHS (every model):** for every muscle, our length RMSE over the gait cycle ≤ MyoConverter's + 0.5 mm.
- **W2 MOMENT ARMS (every model):** for every scored pair, our moment-arm RMSE over the gait cycle ≤ MyoConverter's
  + 0.1 mm (Station 3's M1 tolerance).
- **W3 JOINT MOMENTS (every model):** for every hip, knee, ankle and lumbar coordinate, our moment RMSE over the gait
  cycle ≤ 0.02 N·m/kg (1–4% of typical peak hip, knee and ankle moments in walking) and ≤ MyoConverter's
  + 0.005 N·m/kg.

As the plan allows, a muscle, pair or coordinate that misses W1–W3 may be listed with a reason, but only a reason
shown by a test.

Reported, no gate: max errors; means over muscles, pairs and coordinates; the pelvis (residual) forces and moments;
level 3. If W1–W3 fail, the model stays at Station 5; the cause is fixed in the converter as its own change (logged,
with a before/after on both models). The gates don't change.

## Addendum 2026-10-12: Rajagopal 2016 (written before any Station 5 result for it)

Rajagopal2016 is checked with the method and gates W0–W3 above; nothing above changes. What differs:

- **MyoConverter column:** the cvt3 of the full pipeline run by the Station 1a port (commit 1670ae3), as in the
  Station 3 and 4 addenda (MyoConverter never shipped a Rajagopal 2016 conversion), run in MuJoCo 3.15.
- **Walking data:** OpenSim's own Rajagopal pipeline in opensim-models @ d9b05d4 (`Pipelines/Rajagopal`) ships the
  raw trial (markers, ground reaction forces) but not its inverse kinematics, so `station5/make_rajagopal_trial.py`
  rebuilds it with OpenSim's tools and the pipeline's own settings: the generic model scaled with the pipeline's
  applied scale factors and given its subject marker set, then the InverseKinematicsTool with `IK/ik_setup_walk.xml`.
  Ground reaction forces: `ID/grf_walk.xml` with `ExpData/grf_walk.mot`. Time range: the ID setup's 0.45–1.8 s. As
  for the gait models, the coordinates are replayed on the generic model.
- **Level 3 is not run:** the pipeline ships no CMC result (excitations) for this trial.

Expected: the MyoConverter column carries Station 2's knee error (15.6 mm below the knee), so its lengths, moment arms
and joint moments below the knee should be much worse than ours; W3's absolute limit (0.02 N·m/kg) is the harder part
for both, because the generic model is driven by a scaled subject's data and Rajagopal's light feet and patellae make
the ankle and knee moments sensitive.

## Amendment 2026-10-12 (Rajagopal 2016 only; before any Station 5 result for it)

Station 3 found that for paths wrapping over cylinders OpenSim's `computeMomentArm` is not the derivative of OpenSim's
own muscle length (Rajagopal 2016: typically 0.05–1 mm, up to 18 mm off; station3/GATES.md, amendment of the same
day). The same definition change applies here, for Rajagopal2016 only: OpenSim's moment arm along the trial is −dL/dq
of OpenSim's muscle length by central difference (h = 1e-6, coupled coordinates following their constraint, as
MuJoCo's side), and W0's OpenSim self-check compares h with 10h within 1e-6 m on ≥ 99% of samples. `computeMomentArm`
is still stored and its difference reported. Finite differences now also move coupled coordinates with their
constraint (Rajagopal's knee_angle_beta; the gait models have no couplers, and their reference data are unchanged
bit for bit). Seen before writing this: no Station 5 number for Rajagopal2016.

## Amendment 2026-10-13 (Rajagopal 2016 only; after W0 failed in run1, before any W1–W3 number)

run1 failed W0 for our column on the check itself, not on a converted value (W1–W3 were not evaluated, so no W1–W3
number exists for any column): (1) "OpenSim coordinates without a MuJoCo joint: knee_angle_r_beta, knee_angle_l_beta",
the coordinates Rajagopal's patella couplers drive, which the converter reproduces through the coupling (helper
joints, change 2.4) instead of as joints of their own; (2) OpenSim QErr 2.0e-9 > 1e-9, because the IK file's own
column for those coordinates satisfies the coupler only to IK's accuracy. Stations 2 and 3 already treat coupled
coordinates as following their constraint. Changes, which leave the gait models (no couplers) bit for bit unchanged:
- A coordinate an enforced CoordinateCouplerConstraint drives needn't have a MuJoCo joint (W0).
- Along the trial its value, speed and acceleration come from the coupler and the independent coordinates (chain
  rule), not from the IK file's column; QErr's limit stays 1e-9.
- Its generalized force from inverse dynamics is carried to its independent coordinate(s) by the coupler's derivative
  (virtual work), which is what the MuJoCo side's projection onto the coordinates does; W3 then compares the
  independent coordinates (knee_angle_r/l include the patella's share on both sides). The solver's own output is kept
  (`tau_raw`).
Everything else (gates, tolerances, columns, data) is unchanged. Seen before writing this: run1's W0 problems above;
no W1–W3 number.
