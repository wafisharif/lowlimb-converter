# Station 4 gates: muscle forces

Written 2026-10-10, before any Station 4 code existed or had run. A copy was posted to the plan doc (version history
timestamps it) before any run.

**Question:** hold the model still in a pose, activate a muscle: does the converted MuJoCo muscle pull with the same
force as the OpenSim muscle? OpenSim's gait muscles have elastic tendons (Millard2012EquilibriumMuscle in
gait10dof18musc, Thelen2003Muscle in gait2354); MuJoCo's have rigid ones. MyoConverter's Step 3 fits each MuJoCo
muscle's force-length parameters to OpenSim's, at full activation only, on its own joint grid. This station checks the
result independently, at several activation levels, and compares our converter with MyoConverter's output.

## Models

gait10dof18musc and gait2354 (both passed Stations 2 and 3).

| Column | MuJoCo file | Run in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MyoConverter (baseline) | `baseline/<model>/<model>_cvt3.xml` (Station 0 output) | MuJoCo 2.3.7 |
| Ours | cvt3 from the full pipeline with the current converter, regenerated by the run script | MuJoCo 3.15 |

## Method (fixed now)

- **Poses:** the Station 2 poses (1000 random + 41-point sweeps; seed 20261006), set as in Stations 2–3.
- **Activation levels:** requested a ∈ {0, 0.25, 0.5, 1}, the same for every muscle at once. OpenSim may raise the
  requested activation to the muscle's minimum activation; the value OpenSim actually holds is recorded per muscle
  and the MuJoCo muscle gets exactly that value.
- **OpenSim force:** all speeds zero, `Model.equilibrateMuscles`, then `Muscle.getTendonForce` (the force the muscle
  applies to the bones; equal to `getFiberForceAlongTendon` at equilibrium).
- **MuJoCo force:** qvel = 0, the muscle's activation state set as above, forward dynamics computed, force =
  −`actuator_force` (MuJoCo muscles pull with negative force).
- **Error:** per muscle and activation level, RMS and max |force difference| over the poses, as % of OpenSim's
  max isometric force (Fmax).

## Gates

- **F0 THE CHECK IS VALID (every model, both columns):** every OpenSim muscle has a MuJoCo muscle actuator of the same
  name; pose count is 1000 + 41 × (swept coordinates); MuJoCo's equality residual ≤ 1e-9 and OpenSim's QErr ≤ 1e-9;
  every MuJoCo force is finite; for each muscle and activation level, OpenSim's equilibrium gives a finite force on
  ≥ 99% of poses, and |tendon force − fiber force along tendon| ≤ 1e-6 × Fmax on ≥ 99% of poses. Samples where
  OpenSim gives no finite force are left out of both columns and counted. If F0 fails, F1–F2 are not evaluated.
- **F1 FULL ACTIVATION (every model):** for every muscle, our RMS force error at a = 1 is ≤ MyoConverter's + 0.5% of
  Fmax.
- **F2 PASSIVE (every model):** for every muscle, our RMS force error at a = 0 (OpenSim's minimum activation) is
  ≤ MyoConverter's + 0.5% of Fmax.

As the plan allows, a muscle that misses F1 or F2 may be listed with a reason, but only a reason shown by a test.

Reported, no gate: a = 0.25 and 0.5; max errors; mean over muscles; muscles within 5% of Fmax; how the error
depends on activation (OpenSim's elastic tendon makes force not proportional to activation, MuJoCo's rigid one does).

If F1 or F2 fails, the model stays at Station 4; the cause is fixed in the converter as its own change (logged, with a
before/after on both models). The gates don't change.

## Amendment 2026-10-10 (after run1; see EXPERIMENTS.md)

run1 failed F0 on gait2354, not because of either converter but because of the OpenSim reference: at 1.3% of poses
(soleus_l, a = 1; fewer for 20 other muscles) the muscle is shorter than its tendon slack length plus its minimum
fiber length. OpenSim then clamps the fiber at its minimum length (and warns "at its minimum fiber length"): the tendon
reports a force while the fiber produces none, so no static equilibrium exists and there is no OpenSim force to compare
with. Without this amendment gait2354 can never pass F0, whatever the converter does.

- A sample where OpenSim's fiber length is at its minimum (`getFiberLength` ≤ `getMinimumFiberLength`) is treated like a
  sample with no finite force: left out of both columns and counted, per muscle and activation level.
- F0's two ≥ 99% criteria (finite force; equilibrium residual ≤ 1e-6 × Fmax) are evaluated on the samples that remain.
- Every other part of the gates is unchanged. Clamped-sample counts are reported with every result.

The original text above is not edited. run1 is reported both ways: as gated (F0 FAIL for gait2354) and with this
amendment.

### Correction to the amendment, 2026-10-10 (same day, before any run used it as a gate)

Applying the amendment showed that its wording excluded too much: Millard2012EquilibriumMuscle (gait10dof18musc) also
reports its fiber at the minimum length whenever the whole muscle is slack (13.9% of samples), and there the tendon
force and fiber force are both zero, which is a valid equilibrium with a valid OpenSim force (zero). Only clamped
samples without equilibrium should go. Corrected rule:

- A sample is left out (and counted) when OpenSim's fiber is at its minimum length **and** |tendon force − fiber force
  along tendon| > 1e-6 × Fmax. Clamped samples where the two agree (a slack muscle, force zero) stay in.

## Addendum 2026-10-12: Rajagopal 2016 (written before any Station 4 result for it)

Rajagopal2016 is checked with the method and gates F0–F2 above (with the amendment); nothing above changes. As in
Station 3's addendum, the MyoConverter column is the cvt3 of the full pipeline run by the Station 1a port (commit
1670ae3; MyoConverter never shipped a Rajagopal 2016 conversion), in MuJoCo 3.15. Poses: the Station 2 poses for
Rajagopal2016; all 80 muscles (Millard2012EquilibriumMuscle). Misses may be listed only with a test-backed reason.

Expected: as for the gait models, the refit (changes 4.1–4.3) should beat MyoConverter's particle swarm for nearly
every muscle; muscles whose MyoConverter path is already off (below the knee, Station 2's 15.6 mm error) will be off
in force too in that column.
